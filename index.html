Benalup-Casas Viejas

An Andalusian escape full of sun, nature and history

Welcome to a Hidden Gem

Benalup-Casas Viejas is a warm, authentic Andalusian town where Irish visitors feel surprisingly at home. With sunshine, nature and friendly people, it’s a perfect escape for travellers seeking culture and relaxation.

Where is Benalup?

Benalup is located in Cádiz (Andalusia), next to the Los Alcornocales Natural Park. Surrounded by rolling hills and white villages, it’s a peaceful and beautiful part of Spain.

How to Get There

From Ireland, fly to Jerez, Seville, or Málaga. The town is 1–2 hours away by rental car or regional bus.
